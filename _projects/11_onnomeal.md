A Flask web application that builds personalized daily meal plans from a database of 305 traditional Bangladeshi recipes. It estimates each user's energy needs with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, adjusts for activity level and goal, and recommends the recipes whose nutrition best matches each meal's targets.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/Sh-Ifti/onnomeal-meal-recommender) · Senior Design Project, North South University, 2024
