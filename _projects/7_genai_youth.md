**Cross-Cultural Safety for Youth in GenAI** is a project led by Dr. Tanusree Sharma at Pennsylvania State University, USA. As a Research Assistant on the Bangladesh team, I contribute to a ChatGPT prompt-based study of how teenagers in Bangladesh use generative AI, including study coordination and transcription.
