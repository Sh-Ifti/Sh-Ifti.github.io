This project studies how low-literate women in Bangladesh adopt new technology, and how learning together with family members can help (June 2025 – present).

**Approach**

- Regional workshops with low-literate women
- Custom hardware to measure physiological stress variability during technology use
- A family-based mentoring framework to support learning

**Output:** a manuscript in preparation for ACM IMWUT.
