Welcome! I'm Shahariar Ifti, a Research Assistant at the Design Inclusion and Access Lab (DIAL), North South University, where I work on low-cost wearables and physiological sensing for health.

This site brings my work together in one place:

- **[Publications](/publications/)**: papers, including [Spondon](https://doi.org/10.1145/3811242.3819996), our low-cost stress-sensing wearable from ACM COMPASS 2026
- **[Projects](/projects/)**: research projects on stress, mental health, rehabilitation and mobility, plus my code projects
- **[CV](/cv/)**: my education, experience and skills
- **[Code](/repositories/)**: open-source repositories on GitHub

I will use this blog to write about my research, building hardware on a small budget, and what I learn along the way.
