I am a Research Assistant at the [Design Inclusion and Access Lab (DIAL)](https://sites.google.com/view/nsuhci/home), North South University, Bangladesh, where I work with [Dr. Nova Ahmed](https://sites.google.com/view/nsuhci/home) on Human-Computer Interaction research.

I build and study **low-cost wearable and physiological sensing systems for health and wellbeing in resource-constrained settings**. My work combines custom sensing hardware (ESP32, PPG, GSR, IMU), machine learning on physiological signals such as heart rate variability and skin conductance, and mixed-methods fieldwork with the communities these technologies are meant to serve.

Recent work includes [Spondon](/projects/1_spondon/), a US$46 stress-sensing wearable published at ACM COMPASS 2026, a World Bank-funded nationwide study of student mental health, and a wearable for post-stroke rehabilitation. I also work with researchers at Cardiff University, the University of Leeds and Pennsylvania State University.

**Research interests**

- Human-centered design of technology for underserved populations
- Machine learning that generalizes across individuals (personalization, subject-independent evaluation)
- Wearable and physiological sensing for stress and mental health
- Low-cost health technology for the Global South

I hold a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University (2024). **I am applying for PhD positions starting in 2027** in HCI, ubiquitous and mobile health, and ICTD. [Get in touch](mailto:shahariarifti16@gmail.com) if my work fits your group.
